Ask us

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. They help our website work, stay secure and improve. Here is exactly what we use.

  • Essential cookies are always on
  • Optional cookies need your consent
  • You can change your mind at any time

01What are cookies?

A cookie is a small file that a website places on your browser or device. It lets the site remember you, for example your language or whether you are logged in. Similar technologies such as local storage and pixels work in the same way, and we call them all "cookies" in this policy.

02How we use cookies

  • to make our website work properly and keep it secure
  • to remember your preferences, such as language and cookie choices
  • to measure how many people visit and which pages are useful
  • to improve our content and marketing, only if you agree

03Types of cookies we use

Essential cookies

Needed for the site to function, for example to keep you signed in or to protect forms from abuse. They cannot be switched off.

Performance cookies

Help us understand how visitors use the site, so we can fix problems and improve it. The information is combined and anonymous.

Functional cookies

Remember your choices, such as your selected language, so you do not need to set them again.

Marketing cookies

Used to show relevant content and measure campaigns. We only set them if you give consent.

05Third-party cookies

Some cookies come from services we use, such as analytics, embedded videos or social buttons. These companies have their own privacy policies, and we recommend that you read them.

06How to manage cookies

You can accept or decline optional cookies in our cookie banner. You can also delete or block cookies in your browser settings:

  • Chrome: Settings, Privacy and security, Cookies
  • Firefox: Settings, Privacy and Security, Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences, Privacy, Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings, Cookies and site permissions

If you block essential cookies, some parts of the site, such as forms and login, may stop working.

07Do Not Track and changes

Where your browser sends a "Do Not Track" or Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as a request to decline optional cookies. We may update this policy from time to time, and the date at the top of the page always shows the latest version.
