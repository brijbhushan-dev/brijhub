Ask us

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains in plain language how BrijHub handles personal information when you visit our website, contact us or work with us.

  • We collect only what we need
  • We never sell your personal data
  • You can access or delete your data any time

01Who we are

BrijHub ("we", "us") is a technology company that builds software, cloud, AI and security solutions. For the purposes of data protection law we are the controller of the personal data described in this policy.

You can reach our privacy contact at brijpant88@gmail.com.

02Information we collect

We collect information in three ways:

  • You give it to us: your name, email, phone number, company and message when you fill in a form, request a quote or contact support.
  • We collect it automatically: device, browser, pages visited and approximate location, using cookies and similar tools.
  • From project work: the business data and materials you share so that we can build and support your product.

03How we use your information

  • to reply to your enquiries and provide the services you ask for
  • to send invoices, project updates and support messages
  • to improve our website, content and services
  • to send news and offers if you have agreed to receive them
  • to keep our systems secure and to meet legal duties

04Legal bases

Where the law requires a legal basis, we rely on: performing a contract with you, your consent (for example for newsletters), our legitimate interests in running and improving our business, and compliance with legal obligations.

05Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with:

  • service providers that help us run our business, such as hosting, email, analytics and payment providers, under strict contracts
  • professional advisers such as lawyers and accountants
  • authorities, when the law requires it

If we ever merge with or are acquired by another company, your data may be transferred, and we will tell you before it is handled under a different policy.

06Cookies and tracking

We use cookies to make the site work, to understand how it is used and, with your consent, to personalise content. You can change your choices at any time. Read our Cookies Policy for the full list.

07How long we keep data

We keep personal data only as long as we need it for the purposes above. As a guide: enquiries for 24 months, client records for 7 years to meet tax rules, and marketing preferences until you withdraw consent. After that we delete or anonymise the data.

08How we protect your data

We use encryption in transit and at rest, access controls, secure development practices and regular security reviews. Our team is bound by confidentiality duties. No system is perfectly secure, so we also have a process for detecting, reporting and fixing incidents quickly.

09Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you
  • correct information that is wrong
  • delete your data or restrict how we use it
  • object to certain uses, including direct marketing
  • receive your data in a portable format
  • withdraw consent at any time

To use any of these rights, email us. We reply within 30 days, and you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

10International transfers and children

We work with clients in many countries, so your data may be processed outside your own. Where we do this we use approved safeguards such as standard contractual clauses. Our website is not meant for children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

11Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our practices or the law change. We will publish the new version here with a new "last updated" date, and tell you directly if the changes are important.
