Priority Guide

Which Priority Should I Choose?

Choosing the right level helps us help the right people first.

Critical

Your service is unavailable or there is a serious security concern.

Target reply: 30 minutes
High

A major feature is not working, but some work can continue.

Target reply: 2 hours
Normal

A question or a minor problem with a workaround.

Target reply: 1 business day
Low

A request, an idea or a general enquiry.

Target reply: 2 business days

Reply times are targets and depend on your agreement or support plan.
