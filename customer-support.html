Response Targets

How Quickly We Aim To Reply

We prioritise requests by how much they affect your business. These are our target first-response times.

PriorityTypical exampleTarget first response
CriticalYour service is completely unavailable30 minutes
HighA major feature is not working2 hours
NormalA question or a minor issue1 business day
LowA request, idea or general feedback2 business days

These are targets, not guarantees. Times depend on your support plan or agreement, and on the information you give us. Out-of-hours support for critical issues is available on plans that include it.
