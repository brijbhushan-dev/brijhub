Overview

Security That Fits Your Business

Security is not one product. It is a set of habits, controls and checks. We help you understand your risks and put sensible protections in place, without slowing your team down.

Our work is practical: clear findings, prioritised fixes and plain-language reports that both technical and non-technical people can use.

This service is a good fit for

  • Businesses launching a new web or mobile product
  • Teams that handle customer or payment data
  • Companies asked to prove security to customers
  • Organisations that want to be ready for incidents

Our Approach

A Clear Process, No Surprises

Four simple stages, with regular updates and your approval at each important step.

  1. 1

    Scope

    We agree what will be tested, the rules and the schedule in writing.

  2. 2

    Assess

    We review and test the agreed systems using recognised methods.

  3. 3

    Report

    You receive prioritised findings with clear steps to fix them.

  4. 4

    Improve

    We support fixes and can re-test to confirm the issues are resolved.
