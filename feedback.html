Our Process

How We Handle Complaints

A fair, simple process so you always know what happens next.

  1. 1

    You tell us

    Send your complaint using the form, by email or by phone.

  2. 2

    We acknowledge

    We confirm receipt, normally within 2 business days.

  3. 3

    We investigate

    A senior manager who was not involved reviews the facts.

  4. 4

    We respond

    We explain our findings and any action we will take.
