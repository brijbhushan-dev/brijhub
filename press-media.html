05Using our name and logo

You are welcome to use the BrijHub name and logo to refer to us in news stories, blog posts and partner pages. Please follow these rules:

  • keep the logo undistorted and leave clear space around it
  • do not change its colours, add effects or combine it with other logos
  • do not suggest that BrijHub endorses a product or service unless we have agreed to it in writing
  • for advertising or merchandise, ask for our written permission first

Any use of our brand that could mislead people about our relationship with you is not allowed.
