Ask us

We want you to be happy with our work. If something goes wrong, this page explains when you are entitled to a refund and how we handle it.

  • Refund requests are answered in 3 business days
  • Money is returned in 5 to 7 business days
  • Work already delivered is paid at the agreed rate

01Overview

Our projects are custom work, so we cannot "return" them like a product. Instead, our refund rules protect both sides: you pay in stages as value is delivered, and you can stop at the end of any stage.

02When you are eligible for a refund

  • We did not deliver a milestone that you approved in the agreement, and we cannot fix it within a reasonable time.
  • You cancel before work on a milestone has started.
  • You were charged twice or charged the wrong amount.
  • We fail to meet the agreed 30-day post-launch warranty for a clear defect.

03Project deposits

The advance you pay to start a project reserves our team and covers discovery and planning. If you cancel within 7 days of paying it, and before we have begun any work, the deposit is returned in full. After work has started, the deposit is used to pay for the work already done, and any balance is returned.

04Milestone payments

Each milestone is paid when we deliver it. If you do not approve a milestone, tell us what is missing within 10 business days and we will correct it at no extra cost. A milestone that you have approved is not refundable.

05Maintenance plans and subscriptions

Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time and stop at the end of the current billing period. Annual plans can be cancelled within 14 days for a full refund, and after that for a pro-rata refund of the unused full months.

06How to request a refund

Send your request to brijpant88@gmail.com or use the contact form, and include:

  • your name and project or invoice number
  • the amount and the reason for the request
  • any messages or files that help us understand the issue

07Processing times

We review every request within 3 business days. Approved refunds are sent to your original payment method and usually reach your account in 5 to 7 business days, depending on your bank or card provider.

Bank charges and currency exchange differences are outside our control and may reduce the amount you receive.

08What is not refundable

  • approved milestones and completed work
  • third-party costs we already paid on your behalf, such as domains, licences or app store fees
  • time spent on change requests you approved
  • services used in breach of our Terms of Service

09Disputes

If you disagree with our decision, ask for a review by a senior manager, who will reply within 7 business days. If we still cannot agree, we are happy to use an independent mediator before going to court.
