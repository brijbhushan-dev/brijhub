Ask us

Money matters should never be confusing. These policies describe how pricing, invoices and payments work when you work with BrijHub.

  • Fixed, itemised quotes
  • Clear invoices with no hidden fees
  • Several secure payment methods

01Pricing and quotes

Every project starts with a written quote that lists the scope, deliverables, timeline and price. Quotes are valid for 30 days. If the scope changes, we send a revised quote and begin the extra work only after you approve it.

02Invoicing

We invoice by email in line with the payment schedule in your agreement. Each invoice shows the project, the milestone, the amount, any taxes and the due date, so it is easy to match with your records.

03Payment terms

  • Invoices are due within 15 days of the invoice date unless the agreement says otherwise.
  • A typical schedule is 30% to start, payments at agreed milestones and the balance on launch.
  • Ongoing support and maintenance are billed monthly in advance.

04Accepted payment methods

You can pay by bank transfer, credit or debit card and approved online payment services. We never ask for card details by email. For security, always use the payment link on your invoice.

If you receive a message that asks you to change our bank details, please call us before you pay. This is a common scam.

05Late payments

If an invoice is not paid on time, we send a reminder. After 15 days past the due date, we may charge a late fee of 1.5% per month on the unpaid amount and pause work until the account is up to date. We will always try to talk to you first.

06Taxes

Our prices do not include applicable taxes such as GST or VAT, which are added to the invoice where required. You are responsible for any withholding taxes that apply in your country, and we will give you the documents you need to claim them.

07Currency and exchange

Quotes are given in the currency stated on them. If you pay in another currency, exchange rate differences and bank charges are your responsibility, and the invoice is considered paid only when we receive the full amount.

08Expenses

Travel and other expenses are billed only when agreed in advance. Third-party services that we buy for your project, such as cloud hosting or software licences, are passed on at cost.

09Records and audits

We keep financial records for at least 7 years as the law requires. On request, we can provide statements of account, copies of invoices and a summary of time spent on a project.

For questions about a bill, email brijpant88@gmail.com and we will reply within one business day. See also our Refund Policy.
